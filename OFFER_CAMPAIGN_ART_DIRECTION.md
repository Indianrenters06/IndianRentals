# Homepage campaign carousel

The four offer cards use generated, text-free photographic backgrounds. Headline, supporting line and CTA are HTML over the image, so the copy remains sharp at every screen size and editable in Homepage CMS → Trust Factors → Offers. The image source, destination, and image description are editable there too.

## Art direction

- 1.9:1 landscape composition; subject in the right half; calm, dark negative space on the left for live copy.
- Premium product photography with warm controlled highlights, charcoal surroundings, realistic materials and no invented logos, UI, prices or printed text.
- Use the IndianRenters warm yellow (`#ffcf46`) only as a small accent in the interface, not as a full image background.
- Keep the subject visible after a center crop to the desktop 2-card carousel and the narrower mobile card.

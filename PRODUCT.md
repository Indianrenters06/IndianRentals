# IndianRenters storefront

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users and purpose
People and teams renting technology in India; customers contacting the rental team or an existing branch. The storefront supports catalogue browsing, rental enquiries and customer support.

## Capabilities and constraints
Existing Next.js storefront, separate Next.js admin, Express/Mongoose CMS. Contact redesign is a separate, non-submitting demo; the current contact layout stays in place. Banner appearance controls preserve image geometry and default presentation.

## Brand commitments
Use the existing Mona Sans typography, yellow accent, neutral surfaces and Heroicons/Phosphor icons. Source business details from the parent repository's `00_brand/company-facts.md`; visual authority is `00_brand/visual-brain.md` and the existing frontend tokens. Do not fabricate proof or branch locations.

## Evidence on hand
`frontend/src/config/locations.js` contains verified branch contacts and coordinates. Existing city illustrations and rental process illustrations are available locally.

## Open decisions
Contact demo initially prioritizes new enquiries with visible customer-support paths; this is a working assumption pending user preference and demo review. Adoption of the demo has not been requested.

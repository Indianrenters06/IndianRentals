A compact black mirrorless camera rests beside the laptop and one muted brand-yellow (#ffcf46) fabric equipment pouch adds the only strong color accent. Minimal contemporary studio, directional window light, warm natural skin tones, deep controlled shadows, tangible aluminum and fabric textures, restrained cinematic editorial photography. Unbranded devices, text-free and watermark-free; render the photograph only, edge-to-edge, without website layout or decorative frame.
